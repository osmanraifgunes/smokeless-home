Frequently asked questions

Is Smokeless free?

Yes. Smokeless is free on iPhone, iPad and Android. It shows ads, but never on the craving support, recovery or quit-support screens.

Does counting cigarettes really help me smoke less?

Counting turns an automatic habit into a conscious choice. Smokeless pairs it with a daily target that keeps going down and with support for every craving, so counting becomes a path to zero.

Do I need an account? Where is my data stored?

No account is needed. Everything stays on your device, and you can export or restore a local backup at any time.

Is there a home screen widget?

Yes. On iPhone and Android the widget shows your smoke-free time and today's count, and lets you record with one tap.

Can Smokeless replace medical treatment?

No. Smokeless is a self-help tool. A doctor or pharmacist can offer treatments that roughly double your chance of quitting for good.
